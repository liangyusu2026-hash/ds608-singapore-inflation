# Key Findings

- Peak headline year-on-year inflation since 2019 was 7.49% in September 2022.
- In the 2019-2023 period, the top contributing divisions in the peak headline month (September 2022), using 2019 basket weights, ranked as follows: Transport contributed 3.43 pp; Housing & Utilities contributed 1.53 pp; Food contributed 1.48 pp.
- In the 2025 onward period, the top contributing divisions in the latest available CPI month (August 2026), using 2024 basket weights, ranked as follows: Transport contributed 1.07 pp; Food contributed 0.46 pp; Housing & Utilities contributed 0.39 pp.
- The CPI basket changed in 2024, so contribution values in these periods are not fully comparable; across periods, comparisons are limited to inflation rates and category rankings, not contribution sizes.
- Headline year-on-year inflation in the latest available month (August 2026) was 2.35%.
- In 2026 1H, year-on-year All Items inflation was 1.20% for the Lowest 20%, 1.75% for the Middle 60%, and 1.63% for the Highest 20%.
- The low-minus-high gap was -0.43 pp, and the middle-minus-high gap was 0.12 pp.
- The residual was below 0.3 pp in 97.8% of months. The largest absolute residual was 0.39 pp in October 2024.
- The residual is close to zero in all years except 2019 and 2024, which are the years when the comparison crosses a CPI basket change.
- The top three ranking is the same under both sets of weights in the latest months (from April 2026 onward), but differs in some months of 2025 and early 2026, so findings about the latest month are robust, while no category is claimed to be the top contributor throughout 2025-2026.
